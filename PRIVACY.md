# Privacy Policy - Lotus Download Manager
Last updated: <date>. Short version: we collect nothing. There is nothing
to sell, leak, or subpoena.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT: nothing. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
   telemetry, no crash reporting, no ads.

2. DATA ON YOUR DEVICE: settings, queue state, and the files you download
   are stored locally on your device only.

3. NETWORK: the app contacts ONLY the URLs you choose to download (and,
   for the browser extension, only your own device at 127.0.0.1). Your
   download history exists only on your device and is never transmitted
   to anyone.

4. WEBSITE: static pages on GitHub Pages. No cookies, no analytics, no
   external resources. Theme preference stored in your browser's
   localStorage, on your device only.

5. CHILDREN: no personal data is collected from anyone.

6. DATA SHARING / SALE: never, for any purpose, now or ever.

Changes to this policy are posted on this page.
Contact: open an issue in this repository.
