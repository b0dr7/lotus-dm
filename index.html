A free download manager for Windows and Android. Eight parallel segments per file, with downloads that resume where they stopped.

View downloads

Multi-segment

Splits every file into eight parallel connections, then merges the parts in order.

Resume

Pause, close the app, restart - the queue picks up from the exact byte it stopped at.

Speed limits

Cap a single download or the whole queue when you need the bandwidth back.

Browser catch

Windows only. Firefox and Chromium extensions send links from the browser straight to the queue.

One queue

All, Active, Done, Failed. One list with status filters - no folders, no library.

No tracking

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no webviews. Free, permanently.
